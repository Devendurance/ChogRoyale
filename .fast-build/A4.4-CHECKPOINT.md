## Slice
A4.4 play guide and rules reference. Completed as one bounded slice on top of A4.3.

## What works
- `/app/guide` renders inside the shared `/app` layout with the Privy login gate, HUD and five-tab navigation.
- The guide covers the real match objective, crowns, Core win, regulation and overtime tiebreak.
- The arena section shows two side towers and one Core per side, the river, both bridges and the central Rift Ford.
- Energy, deployment territory, forward pockets, hand cycling, the seven-card plus commander deck and the one-commander limit are explained.
- Commander active abilities show the real ability name, activation cost and cooldown for all five classes.
- Chaos Pulses, the Arena Patron relationship, pulse timing and the five Chaos families are explained.
- Private room and unranked matchmaking are listed. No ranked ladder, levels, currencies, rewards or upgrades are claimed.
- The full 20-card ordinary roster comes from the same catalog as the deck builder, with real names, costs, types and descriptions.
- The lobby deck header links to the guide.

## Ruleset sources
- `src/lib/combat.ts`: timeline, energy, structures, arena, crowns, deck and ability economy.
- `src/lib/chaos.ts`: pulse schedule, warning window, families and bounds.
- `src/lib/commanders.ts`: class roles, active abilities and cooldowns.
- `src/components/app/play-guide-data.ts` projects these values on the server. The client view imports no ruleset module.
