# Chog Royale visual overhaul v3: readiness report (slice 1)

Source of truth: `Chog_Royale_Visual_Overhaul_Handoff_v3_CORRECTED/` (README, docs 00, 00B, 01 to 05, manifests, references). Date: 2026-10-25. Branch: `visual-overhaul/v3-slice1`.

Status in one line: orientation code is ready and verified on fixtures. No approved-style art is installed, because no separated approved-style asset exists yet. This is not a 25-card revamp.

## 1. Render architecture (repo facts)

- Phaser `4.2.1`, loaded lazily inside `src/app/dev/battle/BattleCanvas.tsx`. One scene (`Arena`), `Phaser.Scale.FIT`, a fixed 500x800 board at 5 px per arena unit. The canvas is a view only. It never computes damage, energy, targeting or outcomes.
- The same BattleCanvas is used by `/app/play/battle` and `/dev/battle` through `BattlePanel.tsx`. The HUD (`BattleHud.tsx`, `battle-hud.css`) is React and sits around the canvas.
- Board transform for every entity and layer: `screen = (x*5, seat === 0 ? (160 - y)*5 : y*5)`. Seat 1 is a vertical mirror only. Pointer input goes through `screenToArena` in `src/lib/battle-input.ts` with the same seat rule. This is what makes the view player-relative: the viewing player's own side is always the bottom of their screen.
- Asset registries:
  - `src/app/dev/battle/sprite-registry.ts` reads `public/assets/chog-kit/v001/manifests/phaser-assets.runtime.json` (generated by `scripts/import-sprite-kit.ts`). It binds engine ids to static sprites, card portraits and effect primitives, with vector placeholders as fallback.
  - `src/app/dev/battle/arena-registry.ts` reads `public/assets/arena/chaos-crossing/v002/manifests/arena.runtime.json` (generated by `scripts/import-arena-v002.ts`). It owns layer state, mount assembly, defender pose logic and team colours. All pure and unit tested.
- Animation: there are no sprite sheets or frame atlases anywhere. Units are single static PNGs with procedural motion in BattleCanvas (spawn pop, bob, move tilt, attack windup and release, hit flash, death fade), driven by `battle-animation.ts` from authoritative frames.
- Tower defenders: each structure renders a v002 mount assembly (structure base, socket, defender pose, front rim) at the authoritative ground point. Poses: `idle`, `aim`, `fire`, `hit`, `aim-north`, `aim-south`.
- Card UI: HUD cards and `/app/decks` use the v001 `card:*` portrait derivatives (256x256). Card names, costs and copy come from `src/lib/card-details.ts` and the ruleset tables.

## 2. Immutable gameplay invariants (held by this slice)

- Arena 100x160 units. River y 74 to 86, bridges x 24 to 36 and 64 to 76, rift ford x 44 to 56 (`ARENA_V2` in `src/lib/combat.ts`).
- Towers at (30,40), (70,40) for owner 0 and (30,120), (70,120) for owner 1. Cores at (50,8) and (50,152). The six anchors mirror about y = 80.
- Deploy zones, pathing, hitboxes, spawn points, ability radii, targeting, HP and every card value are untouched. No server, protocol, Colyseus, settlement or database change.
- Ownership stays a domain value (`owner` 0 or 1). Display team is derived only from `owner === seat`.
- No spectator or replay viewer exists in the app, so there's no third camera rule to preserve.

## 3. Orientation audit and what this slice changed

Before this slice, seat 0 looked roughly right but seat 1 did not:

| Check | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Structure pennants and halos | cyan friendly, coral opponent | `TEAM_COLORS` blue `#2F80ED` friendly, red `#E0393E` opponent, from `owner === seat` |
| Baked `team-trim` layer | purple painted on the P1 half, cyan on P0. Seat 1 flipped it, so seat 1 saw purple under its own towers and cyan on the enemy, with upside-down flags | Rendered unflipped for both seats, cropped into a top half tinted red and a bottom half tinted blue |
| `foundations`, `background`, `foreground` | flipped for seat 1, so tower pads sat upside down under upright mounts and the near and far arena edges swapped | Camera-space layers stay upright for both seats (`CAMERA_SPACE_LAYERS`, `layerFlipY`) |
| Defender facing while firing | `fire` pose swaps to a side-facing crossbow for 150 ms | Always `aim-south` on the top half and `aim-north` on the bottom half. Fire is a 1.5 px recoil plus the existing muzzle flash and tracer |
| Defender facing while hit | `hit` pose swaps to a camera-facing flinch | Directional pose kept, with a short warm tint |

Board-coupled layers (terrain, river, ripples, ford, banks and bridges, border scenery, chaos overlays) keep the seat mirror, so the river, bridges and ford still line up with the authoritative geometry. Units are unchanged, including the cyan and coral owner rings under troops.

Changed files: `src/app/dev/battle/arena-registry.ts`, `src/app/dev/battle/arena-registry.test.ts`, `src/app/dev/battle/BattleCanvas.tsx`. Safe fallback is unchanged: missing arena layers or mount poses fall back to the v001 structure sprite or vector placeholder.

1. Stage 1 arena pack (unblocks the playable arena slice). 2. Team defender mounts (unblocks Stage 4 tower identity). Two new mount roles or skins, each with `structure-base`, `socket-platform`, `front-rim` and `idle`, `aim-north` (back view, weapon up), `aim-south` (front view, weapon down) at 256x320, pivot (0.5, 0.875), display scale 0.5, plus measured weapon anchors. Red set uses Bulwark identity (gold Chog helm, tall crimson crest, round star shield). Blue set uses Chog Guardian identity (blue armour, teal spikes, round shield). Optional `fire` and `hit` frames must keep the same facing as the matching aim pose, or they stay unused.
3. Character atlases (unblocks Stage 2). Choglings first, then Boulderback: transparent frames for the states the renderer actually uses today (idle, move, attack, hit, death), one 3/4 view (the renderer only mirrors on X), shared foot pivot, documented display scale against the current 192x256 frame and `displayScale()`.
4. Approved-style card art for the same two units at the existing 256x256 portrait contract, checked at 72x96 and 46x61.
5. Owner decision: should troop owner rings move from cyan and coral to the same blue and red? Left unchanged here to keep the slice inside the tower lock.
