# Chog Royale visual overhaul v3: readiness report (slice 1)

Source of truth: `Chog_Royale_Visual_Overhaul_Handoff_v3_CORRECTED/` (README, docs 00, 00B, 01 to 05, manifests, references). Date: 2026-10-25. Branch: `visual-overhaul/v3-slice1`.

Status in one line: orientation code is ready and verified on fixtures. No approved-style art is installed, because no separated approved-style asset exists yet. This is not a 25-card revamp.

## 1. Render architecture (repo facts)

- Phaser `4.2.1`, loaded lazily inside `src/app/dev/battle/BattleCanvas.tsx`. One scene (`Arena`), `Phaser.Scale.FIT`, a fixed 500x800 board at 5 px per arena unit. The canvas is a view only. It never computes damage, energy, targeting or outcomes.
- The same BattleCanvas is used by `/app/play/battle` and `/dev/battle` through `BattlePanel.tsx`. The HUD (`BattleHud.tsx`, `battle-hud.css`) is React and sits around the canvas.
- Board transform for every entity and layer: `screen = (x*5, seat === 0 ? (160 - y)*5 : y*5)`. Seat 1 is a vertical mirror only. Pointer input goes through `screenToArena` in `src/lib/battle-input.ts` with the same seat rule. This is what makes the view player-relative: the viewing player's own side is always the bottom of their screen.
- Asset registries:
  - `src/app/dev/battle/sprite-registry.ts` reads `public/assets/chog-kit/v001/manifests/phaser-assets.runtime.json` (generated by `scripts/import-sprite-kit.ts`). It binds engine ids to static sprites, card portraits and effect primitives, with vector placeholders as fallback.
  - `src/app/dev/battle/arena-registry.ts` reads `public/assets/arena/chaos-crossing/v002/manifests/arena.runtime.json` (generated by `scripts/import-arena-v002.ts`). It owns layer state, mount assembly, defender pose logic and team colours. All pure and unit tested.
- Animation: there are no sprite sheets or frame atlases anywhere. Units are single static PNGs with procedural motion in BattleCanvas (spawn pop, bob, move tilt, attack windup and release, hit flash, death fade), driven by `battle-animation.ts` from authoritative frames.
- Tower defenders: each structure renders a v002 mount assembly (structure base, socket, defender pose, front rim) at the authoritative ground point. Poses: `idle`, `aim`, `fire`, `hit`, `aim-north`, `aim-south`.
- Card UI: HUD cards and `/app/decks` use the v001 `card:*` portrait derivatives (256x256). Card names, costs and copy come from `src/lib/card-details.ts` and the ruleset tables.

## 2. Immutable gameplay invariants (held by this slice)

- Arena 100x160 units. River y 74 to 86, bridges x 24 to 36 and 64 to 76, rift ford x 44 to 56 (`ARENA_V2` in `src/lib/combat.ts`).
- Towers at (30,40), (70,40) for owner 0 and (30,120), (70,120) for owner 1. Cores at (50,8) and (50,152). The six anchors mirror about y = 80.
- Deploy zones, pathing, hitboxes, spawn points, ability radii, targeting, HP and every card value are untouched. No server, protocol, Colyseus, settlement or database change.
- Ownership stays a domain value (`owner` 0 or 1). Display team is derived only from `owner === seat`.
- No spectator or replay viewer exists in the app, so there's no third camera rule to preserve.

## 12. Slice 3: first animated Chogs (2026-10-25)

Masters and `.blend` files stay in `ChogRoyale-Assets/chog-units-v001/masters`.
- Regenerate: `blender.exe -b --factory-startup --python build_units.py -- <masters>` (about 2.5 minutes for both), then `node pack_units.cjs <masters> <out> [review]` and copy `<out>` to `public/assets/chog-units/v001`. `PREVIEW=1` renders cheap identity stills only and `CARDONLY=1` renders only the identity still and card portrait.
- Choglings: cream body, big cream face mask on a cyan head, a sunburst spike ring that runs down past the cheeks, a backswept rear fan and crown spikes, white-ring eyes with large black pupils, mischievous V brows, pink blush, a small smile, short limbs and round toed feet.
- Boulderback: cream face mask with the same eyes, stern brows and big blush inside dark rocky spikes, a charcoal body, layered warm-grey boulder pauldrons, stone upper-arm and forearm bands, two chest plates and a belly slab, a boulder hump on the back, big cream fists and toed feet.
- Directions: one front three-quarter view facing right, mirrored on X by the renderer, which is the existing single-view contract. Units walking up the screen still face the camera.
- Clips (frames at fps): Choglings idle 6@8, move 8@14, attack 6@16 (impact frame 3), hit 3@14, defeat 8@14. Boulderback idle 6@6, move 8@9, attack 8@11 (impact frame 4, with stone chips), hit 4@12, defeat 9@10 (kneel, slump and crumble). Swing period and reach come from `TROOP_CARDS_V1` (800 ms and 1200 ms, reach 2) and a test checks them against the live ruleset.
- Sheets: Choglings 896x448 (112x112 cells, pivot 56,94), Boulderback 1280x880 (160x176 cells, pivot 80,126). About 600 KB on disk and 6.1 MB of RGBA texture for both. Board heights are 34 and 48 px against 27.6 and 41 px for the v001 sprites.

Procedural bob, tilt and attack push are skipped for animated units, while the deploy pop, facing flip and hit tint stay. Spawned Choglings get per-entity loop offsets so a group never moves in lockstep.

Completes the animated troop roster on the Slice 3.1 pipeline. Choglings and Boulderback are unchanged (their sheets and portraits repack byte for byte). Arena, towers, mechanics, server and multiplayer untouched.

- Sources: `build_roster.py` (the ten units and their clips) and `roster_lib.py` (shared materials including Voronoi molten cracks and translucent glow, lathe, tube, ribbon, crystal and slab builders, the Chog base body, head and face, a generalised `spike_fan()` and idle, walk, flight, hit and topple clip generators). Both reuse `build_units.py` for the rig, pose, face, camera, lights, render loop and card render. `build_units.py` gained a main guard, wing joints in the mirror table, and `rig["mirror_geo"]`: the rear view mirrors the whole model (negative X scale on the yaw root) instead of only the pose, so a one-handed prop stays in the hand that carries the pose.
- Units: Chaos Archer (purple fan, leather vest over purple tunic, quiver, recurve bow with drawn and slack string and a nocked arrow hidden after release), Frostshell (crystal crown, faceted ice plates, shoulder clusters, hexagonal ice shield, crystals shatter on defeat, frost burst on the slam), Rift Runner (teal and purple swept crest, purple scarf, wraps), Sparkcaster (gold fan, black robe with gold trim, cape, medallion, staff with a glowing orb that swells and crackles on the cast), Chog Guardian (approved defender palette: blue spikes and scarf, steel plate, riveted round shield, troop punch and shield push), Hexwing (bat wings, tail, claws, flies), Rift Sprites (cyan to violet spikes, translucent glowing wings, glowing toes, flies), Gale Netter (green tunic, harness, bedroll, net launcher with a wireframe rope net that leaves on the shot), Riftbreaker (black and orange spikes, dark armour, molten-cracked stone hammer swung over the top onto the ground, glowing gauntlets and knee guards), Ember Mauler (bare chest under a crossed harness with an ember gem, two huge magma gauntlets, double-fist slam with embers).
- Mechanics: attack windows come from `TROOP_CARDS_V1` (every swing ends inside one attack interval). Ranged and magic units play their draw, cast or shot when stationary with a foe in ruleset range. The runtime projectile sprites are unchanged. Hexwing and Rift Sprites hover over their ground pivot with wing-beat idle and flight loops and drop on defeat. The renderer's existing air lift and shadow still apply. No ability was added.
- Runtime: the manifest adds `hitsAir` and `air` from the ruleset. `engagedFoe()` counts flying foes only when `hitsAir` is true, so archers, Sparkcaster, Hexwing and Gale Netter swing at air targets and ground-only units still ignore them. `UNIT_CARD_PORTRAITS` covers all twelve troops with the v001 portrait as fallback. View selection, rings, hit fx and fallbacks are unchanged.
- Packer: `trim` units are cropped to the union of painted pixels over every frame and view plus a 6 px margin before the Lanczos downsample, and the pivot moves with the crop. Render px per unit is set so `displayScale` is 0.97 to 1.05.
- Sheets: twelve sheets total 16.5 MB RGBA (Slice 3.1: 4.6 MB for two) and 3.2 MB on disk, plus 0.75 MB of 256x256 portraits. New sheets are 0.6 to 1.8 MB RGBA each. All twelve load at scene start because the opponent's deck isn't known in advance.
- Cards: 1024 masters framed like the Slice 3.1 cards, downsampled to 256 and checked at native 72x96 and 46x61.
- Below the concept boards and cards: still primitive rigid parts with no sculpting or painted textures. Faces look forward rather than turned three-quarter like the cards. Rear views read by colour and silhouette. Riftbreaker's orange rear blades are louder than the card's orange accents. Rift Runner's scarf tail is mostly hidden from the front. Sparkcaster's gold cape hem reads as a solid wedge from behind. Hexwing's wings blend a little into the purple card background. Gale Netter's launcher is a pole and hoop rather than the card's crossbow frame. Archer draw and release and the Netter shot are small at 390x844. Faces and props read at true size, expressions don't. The app shell deck screens (`src/app/app/catalog.ts`) still show their existing site and sprite art.
