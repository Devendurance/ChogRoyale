# CHOG ROYALE — FULL ART BIBLE v3
## Approved aesthetic: premium, original, bright, stylized 3D fantasy rendered for a 2.5D Phaser game

> **v3 identity correction:** This art bible is governed by `docs/00B_CHOG_IDENTITY_LOCK.md`.
> The approved 3D concepts establish **quality, render richness, lighting, materials, and overall appeal**.
> The original 25-card roster remains the **canonical identity anchor**.
> Therefore: preserve the Chog look and commander/NFT recognizability first; apply premium polish second.

### A0. Identity-preservation summary
- Keep units recognizably themselves.
- Keep Chogs feeling like Chogs.
- Keep commanders recognizable as their NFT/commander selves.
- Preserve signature accessories, motifs and silhouette anchors.
- Do not genericize units in pursuit of polish.

### A. Brand art statement
Chog Royale is a playful high-impact fantasy battler with a distinctive family of Chog creatures. Characters must look collectible and charismatic in 3D-style marketing art **and remain readable as small active sprites**. Borrow broad principles of top-tier mobile game illustration (exaggeration, silhouettes, shape economy, crisp material shading), not identifiable competitor character shapes, equipment or exact visual assets.

### B. Art pillars
1. **Silhouette first:** every troop distinguishable at battlefield display size without relying exclusively on tint.
2. **Chog species continuity:** big expressive eye language, stylized bold head-spikes/quills, confident little body language; not all faces identical.
3. **Bright, toy-like material polish:** forms are chunky, round-beveled, hand-authored, with convincing light, not flat vectors and not gritty realism.
4. **Gameplay clarity:** spells/tower states and allegiance visible against green grass in motion.
5. **Visual rhythm:** one dominant shape, one secondary prop/costume silhouette, one restrained accent/VFX signature for each unit.

### C. Common proportions, colors and lighting
- **Overall:** 2.5–3.5 head-height stylization as a *starting range*, but adapt to unit role; commanders may be taller and tanks broader. This is an art ratio, not a gameplay collision target.
- **Shapes:** rounded soft Chog anatomy; square masses for defense; forward triangles for mobility; crystalline facets for frost/rift; controlled jagged rhythms for chaos/ember.
- **Eyes/faces:** oversized but differentiated eye shape and brow attitude; faces must read in an 8–12px display-height test when possible. Preserve source palette and recognizable head markings.
- **Materials:** matte ivory/cream skin with subtle warm subsurface feel; satin cloth; broad clean metal highlights; sculpted rough rock; transparent-looking cyan ice; volumetric-looking but efficiently pre-rendered magical glows. Avoid photoreal scratches, hair strands, tiny decals.
- **Key light:** soft warm sun from upper-left of composition. Cool sky fill. Delicate contact shadow. High local color, no muddy black shadows, no heavy vignette.
- **Rendering:** 3/4 elevated orthographic/perspective-look framing consistent with arena view; game-view sprite proportions override poster poses.
- **Asset transparency:** premultiplied/straight alpha behavior must be checked in the actual runtime; avoid bright/black fringes.
- **Mood:** mischievous, energetic, heroic, whimsical. Drama comes from poses and light, not darkness.

### E. Family hierarchy
- **12 troop cards**: playful/clear enough for repeated on-field units; prioritize readability and affordable animation payload.
- **4 deployable building cards**: chunkier silhouette, clear footprint/activation state, recognizable elevation and material family.
- **4 spell cards**: readable cast area, consistent startup/impact/cooldown VFX, never obscure HP or target selection.
- **5 commander-set cards (provisional grouping from supplied roster display)**: Bulwark, Reaver, Deadeye, Scout, Warden; higher costume authority while respecting real-game abilities discovered in repo.

## F. Every card — non-negotiable identity, redesign direction, game-read

### Troops (12)
**01 Choglings** — Maintain ivory mini-creature, vivid cyan spike crown, large eyes, tiny limbs. Raise head-spike hierarchy, blush, impish grin, eager forward bounce. Primary mass: big round head. Material: velvety ivory skin, satin cyan spines. Battle read: tiny abundant mascot; separate each spawned unit with clear silhouette/foot shadow. Avoid copying a common baby-dragon or goblin face.

**02 Chaos Archer** — Retain purple head-spikes and bow. Redesign into nimble Chog scout with asymmetrical violet shoulder scarf, oversized bow arc, dark leather bracers. Pinched triangular action pose, drawn bow visibly legible even at card size. Projectile: controlled purple streak. Avoid generic human archer anatomy.

**03 Boulderback** — Retain white expressive face framed by dark rocky plating. Broaden shoulder mass, stacked angular basalt plates, short braced legs, massive rounded fists. Make rock appear sculpted and worn, not noisy gravel. Battle read: squat nearly immovable rock tank; weight shown via planting/dust.

**04 Frostshell** — Preserve icy blue faceted shell around a Chog face. Turn shell into coherent shield-like overlapping crystal armor with one dominant back crest and large primary blue facets. Cold specular edges and pale fog rim. Battle read: defensive frost shell rather than a generic crystal spike ball.

**05 Rift Runner** — Maintain teal/violet swept spikes and sleek body. Exaggerate forward rake, angular hair fins, spring-loaded ankle/boot construction, light armored torso. Use light cyan motion streaks sparingly. Battle read: fast melee runner with forward directional triangle.

**06 Sparkcaster** — Maintain bright yellow hair spikes, golden/black costume, glowing fire/spark hand from original. New approved render adds premium asymmetrical caster robe, gold lightning ornament and crackling focal orb. Eyes confident, almost smug. Battle read: radiant mage with clearly visible casting hand and warm electric FX.

**07 Chog Guardian** — Preserve blue armored Chog and circular shield. Widen chest and shield rim, simplify shield emblem, make cobalt plates and cyan highlights coherent. Battle read: reliable armored shield-bearer. Proposed blue-tower visual defender should match this actual Chog identity, though exact tower role must be validated.

**08 Hexwing** — Preserve purple small Chog with big bat-like wings. Lead with two broad wing triangles and tiny round body, violet membranes with fine magenta glow, sly brow. Battle read: clearly airborne, not ground troop with decorative wings. Maintain readable airborne shadow.

**09 Rift Sprites** — Preserve tiny cyan/violet luminous winged Chog; make wings dragonfly/crystal-energy silhouettes distinct from Hexwing, translucent-looking with brightest cyan edges. Battle read: fragile bright flying support pack; do not cover screen with persistent bloom.

**10 Gale Netter** — Preserve green quills and oversized visible woven net. Make net a clear silhouette, tool-carrying springy stance; cloth/leather in olive/tan and matte forest green. Battle read: mobile crowd-control tool carrier. Do not replace net with a bow.

**11 Riftbreaker** — Preserve coal-gray armored Chog and fiery amber gauntlet/segments. Make shoulders asymmetrical, one primary massive charged fist, segmented impact plates with concentrated orange cracks. Battle read: offensive armor breaker, distinct from Ember Mauler by mechanical plating rather than living magma.

**12 Ember Mauler** — Preserve orange-red head spikes and molten gauntlets. Shift towards volcanic creature bruiser: inflated magma fists, rough black stone cuffs, yellow hot fracture cores. Battle read: flame bruiser with compact fiery silhouette, distinct from Riftbreaker.

### Deployable buildings (4)
**13 Rift Sentry** — Preserve stubby cannon tower and blue cyan eye/core. Architectural vocabulary: creamy stone base, warm bronze gimbal, chunky cyan crystalline barrel; strong muzzle direction. States: idle, activation, fire, hit, destruction (where the live mechanics support them). Avoid a mini king tower look.

**14 Decoy Totem** — Preserve mini Chog-faced totem, violet + teal plume, turquoise jewel. Exaggerate toy-like carved body; emblem must instantly read as a decoy creature. States may include emergence, idle lure, hit, destruction. Do not confuse with a movable troop.

**15 Chog Burrow** — Preserve low gray rocky den with central cyan crystals. Round burrow mouth, layered earth/stone lip, subtle grass integration. No tall tower profile. States may include closed, summon/open, impact/collapse. Read as ground spawn point.

**16 Rift Mortar** — Preserve short purple-barrel artillery and squat base. Oversized angled barrel with deep indigo opening, concentrated magenta glow, bright heavy footing. Distinguish against Rift Sentry by barrel angle, footprint and palette. States: tracking/idle, lob fire, recoil, destruction where supported.

### Spells / ability cards (4)
**17 Chaos Bolt** — Preserve instantly recognizable jagged purple lightning icon. In-game FX: brief bright violet arc with light-core and dark magenta edge; one impact flare; do not coat whole board in purple. For ranged lightning, preserve actual targeting logic.

**18 Rally Horn** — Preserve gold/cream curved horn. Impact VFX: concentric gold ring and brief friendly-team accents, clear duration hint only if live game supports buff timer. Keep UI and target indicators readable.

**19 Chaos Comet** — Preserve flaming purple/orange meteor. Visual hierarchy: hot orange core, violet plasma ring, short turbulent trail, satisfying short impact burst and fade. Respect actual projectile speed, damage and radius.

**20 Rift Snare** — Preserve violet cyan lattice/net geometry. Visual hierarchy: flattened ground-trap sigil inside clearly bounded radius; low-opacity center allows units to remain visible. Brief activation flash. Respect actual control mechanics.

### Commander-set candidates (5; verify classification in game data)
**21 Bulwark** — Preserve tiny Chog face in golden/bronze gladiator helm, tall crimson crest and round star shield. Approved 3D version: premium thick gold armor, much bolder shield shape, stance of planted protection. Keep it **Chog**, not a generic human Roman soldier. Proposed red-side tower guard identity. Personality: stern, reliable, honorable.

**22 Reaver** — Preserve purple cap with X emblem, crimson/dark look, aggressive melee gear. Broaden ragged asymmetrical silhouette, chaos-infused not gory; jagged scarf/cape hem and short bright arc weapon. Distinguish from armored Bulwark and caster Warden. Personality: unruly rebel.

**23 Deadeye** — Preserve luminous rainbow visor, vivid violet spiky hair, slim dark ranged body. Focus one readable aiming weapon, visor glint, swept back silhouette. Restrained tech-fantasy, no realistic firearm detailing needed. Personality: quiet sharpshooter.

**24 Scout** — Preserve neutral gray cap, red/orange fringe hair, round specs and casual dark outfit. Build agile explorer identity via a visible compact utility pack or tool, light-step boots and small pointed stance. Should stay clever and understated next to bright commanders; don't redesign into generic soldier.

**25 Warden** — Preserve dark-blue star wizard hat, blue robe, pink bubblegum sphere and blue wand/arcana. Approved 3D treatment: tall eccentric hat, gold star motifs, layered ivory/blue mantle, floating faceted blue crystals. Bubblegum is a deliberate iconic quirk, never drop it. Personality: whimsical master mage.

## G. Differences between marketing art and in-game units
- **Marketing/card art:** hero pose, dramatic VFX, rich costume detail, can be large.
- **Battle sprite:** stable 3/4 camera, compact silhouette, strongly lit, feet/pivot registered to gameplay coordinates, simpler FX. Must be produced specifically, not warped from the hero poster.
- **UI card**: retain exact card identity and legacy name/rarity/cost overlays; new illustration framed/cropped thoughtfully into existing 72x96 and 46x61 style tests (sizes seen in provided comparison, not assumed runtime contracts).

## H. Rejection criteria
Reject any art pass if a signature item disappears (Gale Netter's net, Warden's bubblegum, Bulwark's crimson crest, Chog Guardian's shield), all Chogs get the same face, blue/teal merges with the battlefield, or the result is obviously a copy of an existing competitor hero.
